# 下半｜赛博科幻 · 和风 · 港风民国 · 恐怖

## 五、赛博朋克

**十大场景母型（决定性细节才是"像不像"的关键）**

| 场景 | 英文 | 决定性细节 |
|---|---|---|
| 霓虹雨街 | neon-drenched rain street | 湿沥青把霓虹拉成**竖向**倒影条 |
| 广告全息 | holographic billboard | 巨型悬浮全息在雾中投体积光 |
| 巨型建筑 | megastructure / corporate tower | 玻璃塔挤成峡谷，只见顶部一线天 |
| 暗巷 | back alley | 排气管蒸汽 + 一盏频闪坏霓虹 |
| 管线 | exposed conduit & cables | 走线如血管爬满墙/天花板 |
| 义体诊所 | cyber-clinic / chop shop | 惨白手术灯、悬空座椅、机械臂架 |
| 夜市 | night market stalls | 橙红卤锅蒸汽 + 荧光摊棚 + 塑料帘 |
| 顶层 | penthouse / glass estate | 落地玻璃外云上灯火，室内冷白极简 |
| 贫民窟 | undercity / slum tower | 晾衣绳密度爆表、私拉电线、铁皮补丁 |

**垂直分层法则**：顶部冷科技蓝 → 中部广告暖橙 → 底部脏黄或血红。显式写三层光色，画面立刻立体。

**四套配色**
- 品红·青·紫三元对冲 `magenta-cyan-violet triad, high-contrast RGB split`（主流冲突感）
- 单色红光 `monochromatic red, deep blacks`（恐惧/义眼视野）
- 科技冷白 `sterile cool-white with steel-blue tint`（实验室/顶层/飞船）
- 脏霓虹 `grungy sodium-orange & green, dirty flickering tube`（贫民窟）

**六类光源**：彩色凝胶霓虹 `colored gel neon rim`、丁达尔全息 `volumetric beam in haze`、屏幕光 `screen glow bounce`、钠灯 `sodium-vapor orange`、广告板光 `billboard glow`、车流光轨 `light trails`。
**三层布光一句写法**：`front: neon magenta key; side: cyan fill; rim: cold white; back: haze volumetric; ground: wet street reflections`。

**材质 + 磨损叙事**：`curtain-wall glass with hairline crack`、`rust-pitted metal with rust streaks`、`oily puddle iridescent film`、`rubber & steel-braid cables`、`injection-molded plastic, edge worn white`。
> 任何科技材质叠 2 个损伤词（rust / grime / scratches / peeling / cracked），立刻从渲染图变实拍剧照。

**太空物理锚点**：失重写"发丝衣物向上飘"`hair and cloth floating upward, zero-g`；星球地表写专属地貌（熔岩裂谷 / 冰壳龟裂 / 沙暴 / 气态带纹）；巨构写"尺度参照物"`tiny shuttle against the ring, scale cue`。

## 九、恐怖惊悚（不靠血腥）

**光**：底光 `harsh underlight, hollow eye sockets`、顶光 `single overhead bulb, skull shadow`、黑暗中一小片亮 `tiny island of cold light in total black`、门框剪影 `black silhouette in a lit doorway`、轮廓不明 `rim-lit unknown figure`、频闪 `strobe flicker`。
**构图**：负空间 `vast empty wall, tiny subject`、窥视遮挡 `out-of-focus foreground frame, voyeur gap`、居中凝视 `centered symmetric figure, deadpan stare`、背影 `lone figure from behind, depth of corridor`、镜中错位 `wrong reflection`、角落黑影 `anomalous shadow in corner`。
**色彩**：冷绿医院 `sickly green tile`、灰蓝 `cold desaturated blue-gray`、单点血红 `single crimson accent`、枯黄 `jaundiced sulfur light`、死黑 `crushing black`。

**三条可直接粘的成品段**
- 门缝：`dim hallway, a door ajar exactly one centimeter, thin cold shaft of light, total silence, heavy negative space, grainy 35mm` → 观者站得很近、不愿靠近
- 背影顶光：`lone figure from behind at the far end of a spiral staircase, single buzzing top light, rim-lit no facial detail, oppressive black below, desaturated blue-gray`
- 色彩留白：`a child's doll alone in an empty sickly-green hospital room, one red bow as the only color, dead-on symmetric, cold fluorescent top light, vast blank walls`

## 十、题材混搭禁区（踩了就"四不像"）

| 错误混搭 | 后果 | 正解 |
|---|---|---|
| 赛博 + 古风同画面（无叙事理由） | 元素互斥，出"塑料古装科幻" | 用"义体化古装"作中介：`vermilion hanfu with matte ceramic plating, neon piping` |
| 和风 + 中式园林 | 文化锚点混乱 | 明确选一边，用 02 册具名形制词 |

| 恐怖 + 卡通渲染 | 恐惧失效 | 换 `grainy film, desaturated`，弃 cel-shading |
